Preview over a sample chart. On stream, your real chart shows in the middle.

How saving works
  1. Click Save. The first time, choose banner-config.js in this folder and replace it. After that, Save writes straight to it.
  2. If your browser asks where to download instead, save it as banner-config.js in this folder (the stream-banner folder), replacing the old one.
  3. OBS picks up the change within 5 seconds. You don't need to touch OBS.
  4. Images (logo, promo pictures, QR code) must be copied into this folder first; then type the file name, e.g. logo.png.
